Switching

Languages it may switch to

Ticked languages are the ones this may put your keyboard into. Everything installed in Windows is allowed until you narrow it — useful if you can read a language you never write in, because otherwise a page in it would drag your keyboard somewhere useless.

Used only when a conversation offers no evidence at all — no messages of your own, and nothing remembered.

70%

Higher means the product switches less often and is wrong less often. Below this, it leaves your keyboard alone rather than guessing.

Your data

Everything is stored on this computer: which language you use per conversation, and nothing else. Conversation names are hashed with a random value created when you installed, so the stored file is meaningless on any other machine. No message text is ever written down.
